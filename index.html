03 · Philosophy

No AI. No noise. Just one thing.

No gamified badges. No ten-habit dashboards. No streak-saving tricks you didn't ask for. You choose one goal, you do it, and you tell the truth about whether you did. That's the whole app.

04 · Get started

Available now on Mac.

Download One Thing (.dmg) ↓

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later)

After installing:

  1. Drag “One Thing” into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Terminal (press Cmd + Space, type “Terminal”, hit Enter).
  3. Copy and paste this exact line into Terminal, then press Enter:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/One Thing.app"
  4. Open One Thing from your Applications folder as normal. It will now open without any warning.

This is a one-time step. You won’t need to do it again.
