02 · The daily loop

Pick it. Plan it. Do it.

01

One goal a day

Every day starts with one question: what's today's one thing? Type something fresh, or pick a step from your weekly checklist. Just one — there's no to-do list to hide behind.

  • An independent task, or a step from your week
  • One goal per day, by design
02

Schedule it

Decide when you'll actually do it: today, tomorrow or the day after, down to the minute. Committing to a time is half the work.

  • Any time in the next 48 hours
  • A reminder at that time on iPhone
03

A calm focus timer

Pick a duration and start. The ring empties as you work. Wander off to another app and it pauses after a three-second grace period; come back and it carries on.

  • Pauses when you leave, resumes when you return
  • +5 min when you need a little longer
  • Remembers where it was, even after a restart
04

Done, or not. Honestly.

When time's up, you mark it Done or Failed. No partial credit, no auto-complete. Your streak counts days in a row you got it done — a failed or skipped day starts it again.

  • Two buttons, one honest answer
  • A streak that means something

03 · The week

Plan the week. Live the day.

05

A weekly checklist

Set a goal for the week and break it into steps. Each day, tap one step to make it today's one thing — finished steps tick themselves off. Rather not plan the week? Just do daily goals.

  • Add, edit and remove steps as you go
  • See how far you are: 2 of 5 done this week
  • Daily-only weeks if you prefer
06

Your history

Every day you log lands in History: the date, what you did, and whether you did it. A plain record, newest first — no charts to perform for.

  • Done and Failed, side by side
  • Your streak, front and centre

Timer-friendly apps

Some work happens in other apps. Choose the ones where your timer should keep counting — your editor, your design tool — and switch to them freely. Anything else still pauses it.

  • No permissions needed

08 · Mac

Timer by the notch

While the timer runs, a small black pill wraps around the notch at the top of your screen — a progress ring on one side, the time left on the other. Visible from any app. Click it to jump back.

  • Never steals focus
  • Turn it off in Settings
Your work, in any app
09 · Mac · optional

App Lock

Off unless you turn it on. Pick apps to lock; open one before today's goal is done and One Thing comes straight back to the front. Mark the day done and it steps aside until tomorrow.

  • Only checks which app is in front — nothing inside it
  • No permissions, and one click to turn off
